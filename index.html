About me

I'm just getting started with web development: learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript by building small projects and figuring out how things fit together. This site is both my portfolio and my first real project: a place to track what I'm learning and show the work as it grows.

My path to coding started through my work in education, I care a lot about special education and language equity for English learners, and I wanted to understand the tools and systems that shape how students are taught and assessed. Right now I'm learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript from the ground up, and I'm especially drawn to the idea of eventually building resources that support multilingual learners and more equitable special education practices.
